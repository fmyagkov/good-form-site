Good Form.

Privacy Policy

Effective 25 November 2026

Good Form is published by Otera Health. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. The short version: Good Form has no accounts, and what you log stays on your phone unless you choose to send it to someone.

What we collect

Nothing. Good Form has no accounts and no sign-in, and it does not send your information to us. We run no server that receives your plan, your sessions or anything else you enter.

What stays on your device

Good Form saves the following on your phone so it can show your plan and your progress:

  • your answers to the opening questions (goal, level, equipment, days and minutes);
  • your plan, the session in progress, and every session you have finished, with its sets, weights, how each exercise felt and any note you wrote;
  • your settings, such as kilograms or pounds and whether sounds are on;
  • if you use coach mode: your own details as a coach, your clients' names, the plans you wrote for them and the updates they sent you.

We cannot see any of this, and we cannot recover it for you.

What leaves your device, and only when you send it

Good Form can pack a plan, a single session or a progress update into a link. You choose who gets it and which app carries it (Messages, email, a chat app). The information is inside the link itself and goes only to the person you send it to; it does not pass through us.

  • A session sent to a friend holds the exercises, how long the session took you and how many sets you did.
  • An update sent to your coach holds your finished sessions: what you did, the weights, how each exercise felt and your notes.
  • A plan sent by a coach holds the plan, the client's name as the coach typed it, and the coach's name and any contact details and video call link the coach added.
  • A finishing card shared as a picture shows what is on the card.

Once sent, a link or picture is handled by the app you sent it with and by the person who receives it.

Exercise videos

The exercise videos and their preview pictures are streamed from a video hosting service, Kinescope. As with any content loaded from the internet, that service receives your device's IP address and the address of the file requested. Good Form sends it nothing about you, your plan or your sessions.

Tracking, advertising and analytics

Good Form contains no advertising, no tracking and no analytics tools.

Permissions

Good Form does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, health records or notifications. It plays short tones during a session and does not record. It reads the clipboard only when you tap Paste to open a link you were sent.

Backups

If you back up your phone with iCloud or a computer, Apple may include Good Form's saved data in that backup. Those backups are controlled by you and by Apple, under Apple's privacy policy.

Information from Apple

Apple may give app developers summary statistics, such as download counts and crash reports, from people who have agreed to share them. These do not identify you.

Deleting your data

In the app, open You, then Erase everything. Deleting Good Form from your phone also deletes everything it has saved. Links and pictures you have already sent stay with the people you sent them to.

Children

Good Form is not directed at children under 13, and we collect no personal information from anyone.

Not medical advice

Good Form is a training tool. It is not a medical device and it does not diagnose or treat any condition. If you have a health condition or an injury, check with a doctor before you start.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page with a new effective date. If a future version of Good Form begins to collect any information, we will say so here before it does.
