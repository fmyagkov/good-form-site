Good Form.

Support

Good Form is a strength and mobility app. It makes you a plan, shows every exercise on video and counts the session for you. Coaches can write plans and send them to clients as a link.

For help, questions or feedback, email fedor@oterahealth.com. We aim to reply within two working days.

Where is my data?

On your phone only. Good Form has no accounts and sends us nothing. See the privacy policy.

The videos do not play

The videos are streamed, so they need an internet connection. The written steps under "How to do it" and the counting work without one.

I cannot hear the tones

Check that the speaker button at the top of a session is on, that "Sounds in a session" is on under You, and that your phone's volume is up. The tones play even when the phone is on silent.

How do I change my plan?

Open You, then "Level, equipment and time", and answer again. You can also start any program from the Programs tab.

Someone sent me a link and it does not open

Some chat apps do not make the link tappable. Copy the whole message, open Good Form, choose "I was sent a link" (or You, then "Open a link") and paste it.

How does my coach see my progress?

Only when you send it. On the Today tab, tap "Send update" and send the message to your coach. Nothing is shared by itself.

Can I move my progress to a new phone?

Good Form has no sync of its own. If you restore your new phone from an iCloud or computer backup of the old one, your data should come with it.

How do I start over?

Open You, then "Erase everything". This removes your plan and history, and it cannot be undone.

Is Good Form medical advice?

No. It is a training tool, not a medical device. If you have a health condition or an injury, check with a doctor before you start.
